FAQ

Questions, answered.

Do I need technical knowledge to work with you?

Not at all. We handle everything technical. You tell us how your business works and what's slowing you down — we turn that into automations and train your team to use them.

How long does a project take?

Most single automations go live in 1–2 weeks. Larger systems or custom AI agents typically take 3–6 weeks, delivered in stages so you see results early.

Is my data secure?

Yes. We follow least-privilege access, use enterprise-grade AI providers that don't train on your data, and can sign an NDA and DPA before we start.

What tools and platforms do you work with?

We're tool-agnostic: OpenAI, Claude, Zapier, Make, n8n, HubSpot, Salesforce, Google Workspace, Microsoft 365, Slack, Airtable, and most tools with an API. We'll recommend what fits your stack and budget.

What happens after the automation is live?

Every package includes a support period. After that, you can move to our Partner plan for ongoing monitoring, improvements, and new automations — or run it yourself with our documentation.
